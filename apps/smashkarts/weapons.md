1\. Machine Gun

Common

Rapid-fire weapon that tracks nearby opponents. Excellent for chasing someone from behind.

- Fires continuously for approximately 6.5 seconds.
- Uses a large ammunition supply.
- Damage decreases with distance.
- Works best at close range.

### Important multiplayer considerations

For Yaply, don't let the client decide whether a weapon successfully hits another player.

Your Cloudflare Durable Object should validate:

1. Whether the player actually possesses the weapon.
2. Whether they are allowed to fire it.
3. Whether ammunition remains.
4. Whether the projectile has a valid trajectory.
5. Whether a collision occurred.
6. Whether the target is eligible to receive damage.
7. Whether the player was eliminated.

The client should handle animations, particles, sounds and visual effects, while the authoritative game server validates the results.
